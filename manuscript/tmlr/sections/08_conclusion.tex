\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Uncertainty-aware belief supervision improved mean risk-sensitive lifetime
utility in two controlled manipulation tasks, but its numerical margin did not
transfer cleanly: the inherited Reacher setting missed a frozen trip-rate
tolerance. Target-task development-only calibration recovered that tolerance
on fresh lifetimes without a detectable loss in mean utility. The result is a
two-task simulation study about transfer and calibration under persistent
hidden dynamics, not a hardware or formal safety guarantee.
