\section{Belief supervisor and comparators}
\label{sec:method}

A fixed low-level controller supplies task actions. Above it, a supervisor
selects a high- or low-power mode from an explicit physics belief about the
hidden thermal state. A linear task-boundary transition is fitted only on
development rows,
\begin{equation}
 h_{k+1}=\beta_0+\beta_h h_k+\beta_a u_k+\xi_k,
 \qquad \xi_k\sim\mathcal{N}(0,Q),
 \label{eq:task-transition}
\end{equation}
where $u_k\in\{0,1\}$ is the previous mode. Given posterior
$(\mu_{k-1},V_{k-1})$, the filter computes
\begin{align}
 \mu_k^- &= \beta_0+\beta_h\mu_{k-1}+\beta_a u_{k-1}, \\
 V_k^- &= \beta_h^2V_{k-1}+Q, \\
 G_k &= \frac{V_k^-}{V_k^-+R}, \\
 \mu_k &= \mu_k^-+G_k(y_k-\mu_k^-),\qquad
 V_k=(1-G_k)V_k^- .
 \label{eq:belief-update}
\end{align}
At the first task, $(\mu_0,V_0)=(y_0,R)$. The supervisor selects high power iff
\begin{equation}
 \mu_k+z\sqrt{V_k}<\gamma,
 \label{eq:margin-decision}
\end{equation}
where $\gamma$ is the cutoff and $z$ is the uncertainty multiplier. The
inherited component-level setting uses $\gamma=0.06,z=1.5$; the separately
calibrated Reacher setting uses $\gamma=0.06,z=2.0$.

The experiments also include an optional GRU residual on the physics belief, a
privileged current-state threshold comparator, and a monolithic RecurrentPPO
baseline. The residual receives the sensor, previous mode and its presence
flag, normalized task index, physics mean, and physics standard deviation. Its
mean correction and predicted variance augment the physics belief. The
privileged comparator is diagnostic and is not a planning oracle. The recurrent
result is evidence only about the tested architecture, development seeds,
training budget, and selection procedure.

Exact health is used only to fit and audit development models and by the
explicitly privileged comparator; it is not a deployment input to the physics
or hybrid supervisor.
