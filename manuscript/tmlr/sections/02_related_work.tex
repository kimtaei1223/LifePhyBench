\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Persistent state and reset semantics.}
Action-dependent non-stationarity formalizes environments whose future changes
depend on earlier actions \citep{chandak2022nonstationary}. Continuing and
reset-free robot learning instead studies interaction without ordinary episode,
embodiment, or scene resets \citep{farrahi2025continuing,wu2026cronos}.
Damage-aware manipulation benchmarks accumulate mechanical, thermal, and fluid
health for observation, reward, or termination \citep{balaji2026oopsieverse}.
These precedents rule out broad novelty claims about degradation, health, or
reset-free learning. Our diagnostic uses a different controlled reset: it
restores arm and task configuration while retaining only a hidden internal
health state, which then changes the dynamics of subsequent tasks. The deployed
controller is frozen rather than adapted through continued learning.

\paragraph{Thermal-aware robot control.}
Online motor-core temperature estimation and output limiting, supervisory
thermal protection, temperature-conditioned policies, and residual thermal
control all predate this study and include hardware demonstrations
\citep{kawaharazuka2020thermal,sabelhaus2024supervisory,qian2026thermal,wan2026residual}.
Our thermal law is intentionally less detailed and is neither calibrated in
physical units nor validated on hardware. It provides a controlled hidden state
for testing partial observability, policy composition, and lifetime evaluation;
it is not a thermal-management model for a particular robot.

\paragraph{Hidden dynamics and partial observability.}
History-conditioned adaptation, situation discovery, and action-conditioned
risk gating already address unobserved or changing dynamics
\citep{murillo2026situational,kwon2026adaptive,liu2026riskgating}. We therefore
make no architectural claim for recurrent inference or mode gating. The
experimental distinction is a within-lifetime state generated by the agent's
actions, preserved at task boundaries, and evaluated with the entire lifetime
as the independent unit.

\paragraph{Uncertainty-aware safety filters and calibration.}
Safety filters, predictive filters, belief-space controllers, and latent
filters provide stronger theoretical or full-order precedents for modular
intervention \citep{hsu2024safetyfilter,wabersich2021predictive,vahs2024risk,
seo2025latent}. Recent systems also adapt intervention conservatism from
calibrated uncertainty or probabilistic learned dynamics
\citep{agrawal2026anysafe,frauenknecht2026upsi,kwon2026adaptive}. Consequently,
neither uncertainty margins nor their calibration are novel here. Our
contribution is empirical: fresh-lifetime mechanism attribution, explicit
separation of physics-only and hybrid policies, and a disjoint
development/test sequence. The 2\% trip-rate rule is a point criterion, not a
certificate; the calibration experiment establishes feasibility but not
necessity or superiority.
