\section{Protocol and artifact details}
\label{app:protocol}

The body contains the complete environment update, shared shift definitions,
fitted transition parameters, seed roles, primary contrasts, and statistical
settings needed to interpret the central claim. The released artifact adds the
protocol and checkpoint hashes, condition-level results, full calibration
frontier, recurrent training details, reward sensitivity, and executable
reproduction commands. All generated tables and figures are tied to a tracked
manifest; development, confirmation, and extension outputs remain separated by
seed role.

The main reproduction entry points validate the frozen evidence bundle,
regenerate the paper artifacts from immutable summaries, and run the manuscript
anonymity and citation checks. Exact environment and package versions are
recorded in the artifact manifest. Because appendix review is discretionary,
no result needed to distinguish the physics-only, hybrid, and calibrated-policy
claims is deferred exclusively to this appendix.
